    Scoring

    • Safe tiles score 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7 across the level tiers. Passives score by passive rank: rank 1 is 2, rank 2 is 3, rank 3 is 5, rank 4 is 7.
    • Danger tiles (☠) score 3, 5, 7, 9, 11, 13: the later the fight, the more a death costs, so the more it pays.
    • Line bonuses: +8 per row, +12 per column, +12 per diagonal.
    • A perfect blackout is 385 points. The event runs the full two weeks either way.
    • Ties are settled by a PvP duel.

    Hardcore rules

    • Solo players on one shared server, for two weeks.
    • If you die, you restart as a new Lv 1 character. Completed tiles carry over.
    • Your old base, Palbox and dropped items are off limits after a death.
    • Breeding tech has been removed from the server. Hatching eggs found in the wild is allowed.
    • Contested targets are first come, first served.

    Event conduct

    • Everyone creates their character at the same start time.
    • Each player has 40 hours of playtime for the whole event. The server tracks it, warns you as it runs low, and logs you out when it's gone.
    • Every death stands, including deaths caused by bugs. The server is never rolled back.
    • No exploits, such as clipping through walls or duplicating items.
    • Submit each screenshot as soon as you complete the tile. Holding them back for a late "final submission" isn't allowed.
    • Stealing a Pal someone else is fighting is fair game.
    • Trading Pals, gold and items between players is allowed, and traded Pals count toward Passive and Paldeck tiles.
    • Field alphas must be captured by you. A traded alpha doesn't count.
    • Tower bosses must be beaten solo.
    • Tie-breaker duels have no format restrictions, but the location is agreed before the duel.

    Getting a tile

    • Post a clean end-state screenshot in Discord: a defeat screen, Paldeck count, Palbox or Pal status screen.
    • Passive tiles: five different Pals carrying that passive, shown in your Palbox.
    • Field alphas must be captured: show the alpha in your Palbox.
    • Bounties can be defeated or captured: show the defeat screen, or the bounty in your Palbox.
    • Hunting Season: an inventory screenshot showing a Predator Core.
    • Crude Awakening: the opened big chest with the rig visible around you.
    • Gold Digger: an inventory screenshot showing at least 1,000,000 gold held at once.
    • Condensed Milk: the Pal's status screen showing four stars. Any species counts.
    • OK Boomer: the Pal's status screen showing Lv 60 or higher. Raised or captured both count.
    • Tiles appear on the standings once an admin approves them.
